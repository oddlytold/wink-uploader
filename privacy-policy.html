Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wink Uploader ("the Service", "we", "us") handles when you use it to post content to TikTok.

1. Information we collect. When you sign in with TikTok and authorize the Service, we receive your TikTok open ID, basic profile information (such as display name and avatar), and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues. We also receive the posting options TikTok returns for your account (such as available privacy levels), and the videos, captions and post settings you choose to upload.

2. How we use it. We use this information only to authenticate you with TikTok, show your account and posting options, upload and publish the content you select, and check the publishing status.

3. Storage and security. Tokens are stored securely on the operator's own systems and are never shared. Video files are kept only as long as needed to complete the upload.

4. Sharing. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Content is sent only to TikTok, at your request.

5. Retention and deletion. You can revoke the Service's access at any time in your TikTok account settings. You can also email us to delete any information we hold about you; we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children. The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
