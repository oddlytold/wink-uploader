Wink Uploader

Wink Uploader is a small publishing tool used by the creator of the Wink / Tales, Oddly Told video channel. It uploads the channel's own original videos, captions and post settings to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

The tool only posts content after the account owner signs in with TikTok, grants permission, and chooses what to publish.
